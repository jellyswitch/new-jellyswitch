# A Day Office is a day-pass kind that carries its own room hold

Cowork Tahoe already sells a DayPassType named "Day Office" as an honor-system pass (`daily_limit: 2` standing in for two physical offices — the daily-limit feature's own motivating example), with staff assigning rooms by hand. We decided (David, 2026-08-07) to automate it: **an office-backed day-pass kind whose purchase auto-books a room, rather than a room product that mints a pass.** The type carries a stored **kind** (retiring the `%office%` name-match in `CoverageState#suggested_day_pass_type`, which stays as a kind-based exclusion — an office pass is never auto-suggested as room coverage) plus an admin-**ordered room pool**; allocation takes the first pool room free that date and lives **inside the one purchase authority** (the `CreateDayPass` organizer family), so every entry point — mobile, web, concierge, admin grant, bundle scheduling, door burn — allocates identically, serialized under a lock because reservations have no DB-level overlap constraint. The hold is a **$0 comp-style Reservation spanning posted hours** (no invoice; the SKU price is the money, ADR 0009 applies to office bundles unchanged), which makes the room occupied everywhere, opens its Room Lock via the ordinary reservation gate (ADR 0021), and reverses the ADR 0013/0019 coupling direction as a single deliberate carve-out: this pass creates a booking; bookings still never mint passes. **Room availability alone is capacity** — `daily_limit` is ignored/hidden on office-backed types; sold-out self-serve offers the location's default regular pass instead. `included_meeting_room_minutes` defaults to 0 (office in hand; call-room time bills location overage from minute one, ADR 0012/0025), and the hold itself is excluded from the allowance usage sum. Refunding the pass invoice cancels the hold and rescinds the pass in one motion (amending `RescindForInvoice`'s skip-live-reservation guard, which protects coverage passes, not office holds); admins get a narrow **reassign-room** action (web + mobile, member notified) rather than a general edit-reservation feature; a bundle walk-in with no office free **still burns and enters** — no lockout for prepaid customers — with member+admin notification and the existing admin-restore path as the remedy.

## Considered Options

- **One SKU per office room** — rejected: N rooms become N products, no fungibility, no graceful "any office" allocation, and admin shuffling turns into cross-product surgery.
- **Buyer picks the specific room** — rejected: the pool's fungibility is what lets admins reshuffle freely and lets priority order protect the best rooms; "a private office for the day" is the product, not "Office 3."
- **`daily_limit` stays the capacity gate, rooms assigned best-effort** — rejected: two capacity sources that disagree the moment an admin books a pool room hourly; availability is the truth.
- **No door auto-burn for Day Office bundles (schedule-first only)** — rejected: it stands a prepaid customer at a locked door to protect them from a suboptimal burn; burn-and-notify with admin restore is the humane failure.
- **General admin edit-any-reservation** — deferred: broadly useful but a separate project; the narrow reassign action covers the operational need.
- **Deriving office behavior from the type's name** — rejected outright: the existing `%office%` ILIKE was a tripwire, not a design; behavior comes from a stored kind.
