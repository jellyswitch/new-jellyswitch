# Approval is the hard gate for building access

Follow-up to the Nash incident (ADR 0023): Nash signed up at 1:19 AM unapproved, bought a pass, and was in the building at 1:38 AM — because `users.approved` was enforced NOWHERE in the member-facing product. The mobile app's Welcome screen says "pending operator approval" and even models purchased-but-not-approved, but it only ever occupied the Home tab (Keys/Book stayed tappable), and no server endpoint checked the flag, so the "waiting" state was cosmetic on every surface. We decided (David, 2026-08-08): **approval is the hard gate for physical access — an unapproved account gets no keys and no unlock on any surface (mobile Keys/unlock, BLE auto-unlock, web Keys, dashboard buttons), regardless of day passes, bundles, memberships, leases, or reservations.** The gate lives at the top of both access predicates — `Api::V1::DoorUnlocking#user_can_access_building?` and `Permissions#has_building_access?` (kept in lockstep per the PR #668 list/unlock invariant) — after the staff bypass (superadmin/admin/GM/CM keep access; their flag is irrelevant). The unlock 403 tells unapproved members the truth ("pending approval") instead of steering them to buy a pass that won't open the door. **Purchasing and booking stay self-serve and coverage-gated only** — buy-then-wait is the intended funnel (the concierge widget sells passes to brand-new signups; the Welcome flow already models it); the money is taken, the door waits for screening. The mobile app additionally hides the Keys/Book/Community tabs until approved so the Welcome screen is genuinely where an unapproved member waits.

## Considered Options

- **Gate purchases and bookings on approval too** — rejected: kills the concierge/web self-serve funnel and adds no security once the door is gated; a booking without building access is inert.
- **Client-side lock only (hide tabs)** — rejected as the primary control: any web login or stale app bypasses it; the server gate is the wall, the app lock is the UX.
- **Auto-approve on first successful payment** — rejected: payment is exactly what the screening is meant to precede (the 5 AM thief can pay $40).
- **Gating staff on the flag as well** — rejected: staff are vetted by role assignment; several staff fixtures/accounts predate consistent flag hygiene.
