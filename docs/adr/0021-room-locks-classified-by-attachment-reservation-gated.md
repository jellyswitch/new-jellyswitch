# Room Locks are classified by Room attachment and gated on the reservation, not coverage

Tahoe Longhouse wired electric locks on interior spaces (meeting room, phone booths, studio) into the app as ordinary Doors, which made them coverage-gated: any member could open a $60/h room, any time, and nothing in the model distinguished them from the front door. We decided (grill session, 2026-07-03): a Door **attached to a Room is that Room's lock** — the attachment IS the classification (no exterior/interior flag; operators can always answer "which room does this protect?" but not "is this architecturally exterior?" — TLH's "Meeting Room Ext" is exterior architecture, interior semantics). Room Locks open for the reservation holder during their booking (grace-limited so it can't collide with the prior booking — deliberately tighter than ADR 0013's ±60-min building window) and for staff anytime; an idle reservable room stays locked. Opening a Room Lock is a **Room Entry**, a distinct event from the **door punch** (Building Door open), so Day Pool burn, bundle burn-on-entry, and entry analytics keep their exact "physically entered the building" meaning. Unattached interior locks (walk-up phone booths) intentionally remain coverage-gated Building Doors — booking-gating a 5-minute phone call is friction, not access control; attaching a Room later upgrades them with no other change. Members reach a Room Lock only through their reservation ("the reservation is the key"); Room Locks never render in the general Keys list and are excluded from BLE approach-unlock in V1.

## Considered Options

- **Explicit door-type field** (exterior/interior/room) — rejected: operators misclassify architecture-based labels, and a type without a Room link can't answer *which* reservation opens it.
- **Counting Room Entries as door punches** — rejected: silently corrupts Day Pool ("distinct days used"), bundle burn-on-entry, and the Quiet lifecycle signal.
- **Making every locked interior space a bookable Room** — rejected for walk-up booths: booking friction where none is wanted.
