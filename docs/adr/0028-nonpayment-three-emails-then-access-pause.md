# Non-payment: three emails in short order, then access pauses

Until 2026-09 the platform had NO non-payment consequence: when a member's auto-charged invoice failed, the webhook emailed + pushed them on *every* Stripe retry (Kara Morison at Untethered got 4 identical "Action Required" emails over 2.5 weeks, 8/14–8/27) and nothing ever cut off access — she kept door-punching and booking the whole time. The only cutoff path was Stripe's own dashboard dunning eventually cancelling the subscription, a per-operator setting invisible to the app and documented nowhere. We decided (David, 2026-09-01): **a failed auto-charge invoice triggers exactly three emails — failure notice immediately, cutoff warning ~48h later, suspension notice ~48h after that — and from the suspension notice until the invoice is paid, the member gets no door access and no self-serve room booking.**

Mechanics (see `PaymentCutoff`): each step is a `ProductEmailSend` row keyed to the Invoice, so the webhook dedupes step 1 across Stripe retries and the nightly `PaymentCutoffJob` (Heroku Scheduler → `rake payment_cutoff:run`) advances steps 2–3 only when the invoice is *still open*. **Suspension is derived state** — `User#payment_suspended?` = open invoice + recorded suspension notice — so nobody is ever cut off without having been sent all three emails, and paying by any path (a Stripe retry, member Pay Now, an admin retry) restores access instantly with no flag to reset. The gate sits above every grant in both lockstep access predicates (`user_can_access_building?` and `Permissions#has_building_access?`, PR #668 invariant): membership, day passes, bundles, leases, and reservation windows are all closed while suspended. Booking is blocked by `EnforcePaymentStanding`, first step of both create organizers, flag-gated like its siblings so staff on-behalf booking still works.

Scope (v1): **individual, auto-charged members only.** Org-billed invoices stop after step 1 (the owner gets one failure email), out-of-band/net-30 members are excluded entirely (their invoices sit "open" by design — a prod audit found 16,949 open user invoices, overwhelmingly OOB, which is also why suspension anchors on the drip's send records rather than raw invoice age: deploys can never retro-suspend anyone). Staff are exempt. Existing future reservations are kept, but do not admit the member while suspended. One global 0/2/4-day policy, no per-location toggle.

## Considered Options

- **Configure Stripe dashboard dunning to cancel, keep the app dumb** — rejected: cancellation destroys the membership (member must re-signup), timing is invisible to the app, per-operator and unauditable; the agreed policy is a pause, not an ejection.
- **A `suspended` flag/status column set by the job and cleared on payment** — rejected: two writers (webhook + job) and a missed `invoice.paid` webhook strands a paying member outside; derived state is self-healing by construction.
- **Suspend on raw invoice age (open > 4 days)** — rejected: instantly suspends members with stale/OOB open invoices on deploy, with no warning emails ever sent; anchoring on send records guarantees warn-before-cut.
- **Also cancel/refund the suspended member's future reservations** — rejected for v1: more surprising than helpful; the reservation survives the pause and works again the moment they pay.
- **Per-location opt-out flag** — deferred until an operator actually objects; smallest version ships global.
