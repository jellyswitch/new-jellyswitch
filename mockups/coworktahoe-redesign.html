Cowork Tahoe · Harrison Avenue

The midtown workspace, three blocks from the lake.

Cowork Tahoe opened on Harrison Avenue in 2016 because South Lake didn't have a real place for the wave of remote workers, founders, and consultants who'd quietly decided to make this their home. A decade later, the lake is still here, the slopes are still here, and so are we — now as part of Untethered, our family of Sierra workspaces.

Our midtown location has a quiet, neighborhood feel — three blocks from Regan Beach, walkable to coffee and lunch, free parking out front. Two conference rooms, a full kitchen, a private shower for the cyclists and trail-runners, and the kind of neighbors who'll watch your dog while you take a call.
